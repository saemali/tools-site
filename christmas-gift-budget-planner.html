How to plan Christmas gifts without the January regret

  1. Pick one total first. Look at what you can pay for this month without a loan, credit-card balance or “buy now, pay later”. That number is your budget, not what the shops suggest.
  2. Set aside the small stuff. Wrapping paper, cards, tape, batteries and postage add up quietly. Put a number in “Wrapping, cards and postage” so it doesn’t eat into gifts later.
  3. Share it out before you shop. Press Split by closeness for a fair starting point, then nudge any amount by hand. Deciding at home is much calmer than deciding at the till.
  4. Write the idea next to each name. When a sale comes up you’ll know at once whether it is on your list, instead of buying “just in case”.
  5. Fill in what you really paid. If one gift goes over, the planner shows it in red and tells you how much to trim elsewhere.

Feeling the pressure? You’re not alone

  • It’s okay to spend less than last year. Most people remember the thought and the time, not the price tag.
  • Suggest a family Secret Santa. Everyone buys one good gift instead of many small ones. Our free Secret Santa name generator draws names privately, with a budget on each slip.
  • Give time, not things. A homemade card, cookies, a day out or an advent activity calendar for the kids costs little and means a lot.
  • Black Friday is only a deal if it was already on your list. Check the list before you click buy.
  • Post early. If gifts are going by post or courier, check your carrier’s last posting dates and add the postage to your extras.

Gift budget questions

Is the gift budget planner free?

Yes. There is no signup, no email and no limit. Use it for as many people and as many years as you like.

Where is my list saved?

Only in your own browser on this device, using local storage, so you can close the tab and come back. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. Untick "Remember on this device" or press Clear all to remove it.

How much should I spend on Christmas gifts?

Start from what you can pay for this month without a loan, credit-card debt or dipping into your emergency savings, then work down to each person. A smaller gift you can afford feels better in January than a bigger one you are still paying off.

How does "split by closeness" work?

After setting aside your wrapping, cards and postage money, the rest is shared in the ratio partner 4, family and kids 3, friends 2, work and others 1. Shares are whole amounts that add up exactly to your budget, and you can change any of them by hand.

Can I use it for Diwali, Eid, birthdays or a wedding?

Yes. Nothing in the planner is Christmas-only. Rename the list and use it for any gift-giving season.

Can I open my list in Excel or Google Sheets?

Yes. Press "Download CSV" and open the file in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. You can also copy the list as text to paste into WhatsApp or Notes.

Which currencies does it support?

Indian rupees (₹), US dollars, British pounds, euros, UAE dirhams, Canadian dollars and Australian dollars. The planner does not convert between currencies; it just shows the symbol and number format you pick.
