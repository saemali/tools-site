Thresholds (FY 2025-26 onward): 194J – no TDS if fees paid by a client stay at or under ₹50,000 in the year (raised from ₹30,000 by Budget 2025). 194C – ₹30,000 per single payment or ₹1,00,000 in the year. Without a valid PAN, TDS is at least 20% (section 206AA). From 1 April 2026 these provisions are carried into the Income-tax Act, 2025, but the rates are the same. Sources: Finance Bill 2025 memorandum, incometax.gov.in.
