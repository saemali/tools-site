Thresholds used (FY 2025-26 onward)

  • 194J: no TDS while a client's fees to you stay at or under ₹50,000 in the year (raised from ₹30,000 by Budget 2025).
  • 194C: no TDS if a single payment is ₹30,000 or less and the year's total is ₹1,00,000 or less.
  • No PAN: TDS at 20% (old section 206AA, now section 397(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025).

From 1 April 2026: Income-tax Act, 2025

For any payment or credit on or after 1 Apr 2026, the new Act applies and sections are renumbered: 194C is now section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(i)], 194J (professional/technical fees) is now section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(iii)], and the no-PAN rate (old 206AA) is section 397(2). The Income Tax Department confirms that rates and thresholds remain the same under both Acts. Checked 15 Oct 2026. Official sources: Section 393, Income-tax Act 2025, Section 397, TDS compliance FAQ (transition).

TDS isn't extra tax. It's an advance you claim back in your ITR, so check Form 26AS/AIS. Sources: Finance Bill 2025 memorandum, incometax.gov.in.

Common questions · aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Is TDS a loss?

No. TDS is tax paid in advance on your behalf. You claim it when filing your ITR and get a refund if it was more than your actual tax.

Why did my client deduct 10%?

Professional services fall under section 194J at 10% once the client pays you more than ₹50,000 in a financial year. From 1 April 2026 this is section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(iii)] of the Income-tax Act, 2025, with the same rate and threshold.

Which Act applies from 1 April 2026?

The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies to any payment or credit made on or after 1 April 2026. 194C becomes section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(i)] and 206AA (no PAN) becomes section 397(2). Rates and thresholds are unchanged. Checked 15 Oct 2026 against incometax.gov.in.

Is TDS deducted on GST?

Usually TDS is calculated on the fee excluding GST when GST is shown separately on the invoice.
