Diwali Bonus and Gifts 2026: GST and TDS Explained Simply

Diwali brings bonuses, sweet boxes, gift vouchers and client hampers, along with a few tax questions. Is the voucher taxable? Why is the TDS on the bonus so high? Can a business claim GST credit on the gifts it buys? Here are the answers for 2026, based only on official rules.

Quick answers

Question Short answer Rule
Is a gift or voucher from my employer taxable? Nil value if all such gifts in the tax year total below ₹15,000; otherwise its full value is a taxable perquisite Income-tax Rules 2026, Rule 15(5)(a), Table IV item 4
Is my Diwali bonus taxed? Yes, as salary. Your employer deducts TDS on it Income-tax Act 2025, s.392
Is GST charged on employer gifts to employees? Not treated as a supply up to ₹50,000 per employee per financial year CGST Act, Schedule I para 2 proviso
Can a business claim ITC on gifts? No for goods given away as gifts without consideration CGST Act s.17(5)(h); CBIC Circular 92/11/2019

1. Gifts and vouchers from your employer (income tax)

From 1 April 2026, the Income-tax Rules 2026 value a gift, voucher or token (in lieu of a gift) from your employer at its actual amount. The value is nil if all such gifts in the tax year add up to less than ₹15,000. The old limit was ₹5,000.

  • Below ₹15,000 in total for the year: no taxable perquisite.
  • ₹15,000 or more: the rule values the perquisite at the full amount of the gift, not just the excess. The employer includes it in your salary and TDS.

The limit is an annual aggregate, so a Diwali voucher, a New Year hamper and a birthday gift card all count together.

2. TDS on your Diwali bonus

A bonus from your employer is salary income. Under s.392 of the Income-tax Act 2025 (formerly s.192), your employer deducts TDS at the average rate of tax on your estimated salary for the whole year, and can increase or reduce later deductions to correct any excess or shortfall.

That is why a bonus month often shows unusually high TDS: the bonus raises your estimated annual income, so the average rate is worked out again.

What you can do - Give your employer your investment declarations and proofs on time, under the regime you chose, so they're reflected in the estimate. - If you changed jobs this year, give the new employer your previous salary and TDS details. - Check Form 26AS/AIS after the quarter. Excess TDS is adjusted when you file your return.

3. GST on gifts by a business

Gifts to employees. Schedule I of the CGST Act treats some activities as supplies even without payment, but its para 2 proviso says that gifts up to ₹50,000 in value per financial year from an employer to an employee are not a supply. CBIC's press release adds that gifts above ₹50,000 made without consideration, in the course or furtherance of business, are subject to GST.

ITC on gifts (clients, dealers, employees). Under s.17(5)(h) of the CGST Act, ITC is not available on goods "disposed of by way of gift or free samples". CBIC Circular 92/11/2019-GST clarifies that ITC is not available on inputs, input services and capital goods used for gifts given without consideration. The exception is where the gift falls within Schedule I as a supply.

In practice for a small business: if you buy Diwali hampers to give clients for free, don't claim the GST on that purchase as ITC. If you claimed it and later gave the goods away, speak to your CA about reversing it.

Worked example

Anil, a salaried designer, receives a ₹6,000 Diwali gift voucher and later in the year a ₹4,000 New Year hamper from his employer. His total is ₹10,000, which is below ₹15,000, so the perquisite value is nil. If his employer had also given a ₹6,000 gadget, the total would reach ₹16,000. Under the rule, the full ₹16,000 would then be a taxable perquisite.

Meena runs a small agency and buys 20 sweet boxes with GST for clients. She gives them away free, so she doesn't claim ITC on that GST (s.17(5)(h)).

Common mistakes

  • Treating ₹15,000 as an exemption for each gift. It is an annual total.
  • Assuming that at ₹15,000 or more only the excess is taxed. The rule values the whole amount.
  • Claiming ITC on gift purchases for clients.
  • Skipping your investment declaration, then getting a TDS shock in the bonus month.

FAQ

Is a Diwali gift voucher from my employer taxable in 2026? It has nil value if all gifts, vouchers and tokens from your employer in the tax year total less than ₹15,000 (Income-tax Rules 2026, Rule 15(5)(a)). At ₹15,000 or more, the full value is a taxable perquisite.

Why is TDS higher in my bonus month? Under s.392 of the Income-tax Act 2025, your employer deducts TDS at the average rate on your estimated annual salary. A bonus raises that estimate, so the average rate is worked out again and later deductions are adjusted.

Do companies pay GST on Diwali gifts to employees? Gifts up to ₹50,000 per employee per financial year are not treated as a supply (CGST Act, Schedule I para 2 proviso). CBIC says gifts above that limit, made in the course of business without consideration, attract GST.

Can I claim GST input tax credit on gifts for clients? No, if the goods are given away free. ITC on goods disposed of by way of gift is blocked under s.17(5)(h) of the CGST Act, as clarified by CBIC Circular 92/11/2019-GST.
