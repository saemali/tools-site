How to set up a Christmas treasure hunt (10 minutes)

  1. Make and print the hunt above. Print the answer key too and keep it in your pocket.
  2. Cut out the cards and fold each one in half so nobody can peek. Write the card number on the back if you like.
  3. Hide the cards backwards: put the present at the last spot first, then work up the answer key so card 2 is at the first spot, card 3 at the second, and so on. Doing it in reverse means you never lose track.
  4. Hand over clue 1 and let the hunt begin. Give a nudge (“warmer… colder…”) if a clue is taking more than a couple of minutes.
  5. Count the cards back in at the end so no clue is left in the fridge until New Year.

Tips that make it work

  • Several children? Give them one clue chain to solve as a team, or make one hunt per child with different set codes and a different colour of paper for each so nobody takes the wrong card.
  • Little ones (3–5): choose 3 to 6 clues and 4 big cards per page. They can follow the picture while you read the rhyme. Keep every spot at their height and in rooms they know.
  • Older kids and teens: try the riddles and add a time limit, or hide the last clue inside a wrapped box for a final twist.
  • Safety first outdoors: only use spots inside your own garden or yard, stay with young children, and skip anything near roads, ponds or tools. Use the “Skip spots” list to leave out what you don’t want.
  • Keep the season going: add the hunt to a free advent activity calendar, play Christmas bingo afterwards, or use it for the present at the end of a Secret Santa swap.

Scavenger hunt questions

Is the scavenger hunt generator free?

Yes. There is no signup, no email, no watermark and no limit. Print as many hunts as you like for your family, class or party.

How many clues should I use?

About 3 to 6 for ages 3 to 5, 5 to 10 for ages 6 to 8, 6 to 12 for ages 9 to 12 and 8 to 15 for teens and adults. A hunt with 8 clues takes most children 15 to 25 minutes.

How does the clue chain work?

You hand clue 1 to the players. It leads to a hiding spot where clue 2 is waiting, and so on. The last clue leads to the present. The answer key tells you exactly where to hide each card.

What is different for each age group?

Ages 3 to 5 get very simple rhymes that name the place, plus a big picture so non-readers can guess. Ages 6 to 8 get rhymes with gentle hints. Ages 9 to 12 get riddles with a letter-count hint. Teens and adults get the riddles with no hint.

Can I change a clue?

Yes. Each card has Reword (another rhyme for the same place), Different spot (swap the hiding place) and Edit (type your own words). Changes appear on the cards, the answer key and the copied text.

Can I get the same hunt again?

Yes. Type the same set code with the same settings and you get the same clues. Swaps and edits are not saved, so print or copy the hunt once you are happy with it.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The clues are made by JavaScript in your browser. There is no tracking and nothing is uploaded or saved by us.
