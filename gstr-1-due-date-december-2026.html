GSTR-1 Due Date December 2026: 11 Dec for Monthly Filers, IFF by 13 Dec for QRMP

December is a crowded GST month: GSTR-1, IFF, GSTR-3B, PMT-06 and, for many businesses, the annual return all land in the same few weeks. This guide covers the first one, GSTR-1 for the November 2026 period, plus a checklist and every other December date in one place.

Ghabraiye mat: GSTR-1 11 tak, IFF 13 se pehle (13 ko Sunday hai), aur baaki dates neeche list mein.

The quick answer

The GST portal's GSTR-1 user guide says GSTR-1 is due on the 11th of the month after the tax period for monthly filers and the 13th of the month after the quarter for quarterly filers, unless the Government extends it by notification. QRMP filers may upload B2B invoices through IFF from the 1st to the 13th of the month after each of the first two months of a quarter (CGST Rule 59(2); CBIC Circular 143/13/2020-GST). As of 15 Nov 2026 we found no extension for the November 2026 period. Extensions, when they happen, are usually announced only days before the due date, so check the "Upcoming Due Dates" box on gst.gov.in in the first week of December.

The Sunday problem: IFF on 13 December

13 December 2026 falls on a Sunday. The GST portal stays open on weekends, and we found no rule that moves a GST due date to the next working day. CBIC Circular 143/13/2020-GST and the portal's QRMP FAQ both say IFF cannot be filed after the 13th. So treat Friday 11 or Saturday 12 December as your real deadline, and don't count on Monday the 14th.

Monthly or QRMP: which one are you?

  • Monthly filer: you file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month. Your November GSTR-1 is due 11 December, and GSTR-3B on 20 December.
  • QRMP (Quarterly Return, Monthly Payment): open to taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore. November is the second month of the October–December quarter, so no full GSTR-1 is due. You may upload B2B invoices through IFF (up to ₹50 lakh of invoice value a month, per the circular) so your buyers get credit in their November GSTR-2B. Pay November's tax by PMT-06 by 25 December.

Not sure which one you are? Log in to gst.gov.in and open the Returns Dashboard. It shows your filing preference and your due dates.

Why GSTR-1 on time matters

GSTR-1 is your sales statement. Your buyers' GSTR-2B, and so their input tax credit, is built from what you report. The portal generates November's GSTR-2B on 14 December (GST portal GSTR-2B FAQ), so invoices filed after that show up in your buyer's 2B for a later month. File late and your B2B customers wait for their credit. That is how small sellers lose repeat business. Your own GSTR-3B is also auto-filled from GSTR-1, so mistakes carry through.

What happens if I file late?

  1. Late fee: ₹25 a day under CGST plus ₹25 a day under SGST (₹50 a day), or ₹10 + ₹10 (₹20 a day) if you had no outward supplies. Under Notification 20/2021-CT the total is capped at ₹500 for a nil GSTR-1, ₹2,000 for aggregate turnover up to ₹1.5 crore and ₹5,000 for ₹1.5–5 crore. Above ₹5 crore the section 47(1) maximum of ₹10,000 (₹5,000 + ₹5,000) applies. Work out your figure with our free GST late fee and interest calculator.
  2. Knock-on delays: the portal generally won't let you file a GSTR-1 until the earlier ones are filed, and late filing delays your buyers' ITC.

About the "late fee waiver" posts: the 57th GST Council (8 Oct 2026) recommended waiving the late fee on a delayed return under section 39(1) (that is GSTR-3B) for turnover up to ₹5 crore, if filed by the end of the month it was due. It does not cover GSTR-1 as worded, and as of 15 Nov 2026 we found no notification bringing it into force. Plan as if the normal late fee applies.

Pre-filing checklist (10 minutes)

  1. Export all November invoices, including credit and debit notes for returns and discounts.
  2. Check every B2B buyer's GSTIN. One wrong character sends their credit to someone else's 2B.
  3. Place of supply: inter-state is IGST, same state is CGST + SGST.
  4. Rates: match your item master to current rates. If you sell anything touched by the rate changes from 1 November 2026 (57th Council notifications), check the November bills carefully.
  5. HSN summary (Table 12): 4-digit HSN if aggregate turnover is up to ₹5 crore, 6-digit above.
  6. Document summary (Table 13): invoice series, with cancelled numbers noted.
  7. Totals match your books before you submit. Re-check any doubtful line with our free GST calculator. It runs in your browser, with no signup and nothing saved.
  8. File with EVC or DSC, download the filed return and keep it with your November records. Note anything you missed, to fix through GSTR-1A before filing GSTR-3B.

Tip: the evening of the due date is when the portal is slowest. Filing on the 8th–10th gives you room to fix surprises.

FAQ

What is the GSTR-1 due date for November 2026? For monthly filers, GSTR-1 for November 2026 is due on Friday, 11 December 2026 (the 11th of the following month, per the GST portal's GSTR-1 user guide). As of 15 November 2026 we found no notification extending it.

What is the IFF due date for November 2026? QRMP filers can upload B2B invoices for November 2026 through the optional Invoice Furnishing Facility (IFF) from 1 to 13 December 2026 (CGST Rule 59(2)). 13 December 2026 is a Sunday, and we found no rule that moves it to Monday, so file by Friday 11 or Saturday 12 December to be safe.

I am a QRMP filer. Do I file GSTR-1 in December 2026? No full GSTR-1 is due. November is the second month of the October–December quarter, so you may only use the optional IFF (by 13 December). Your quarterly GSTR-1 for October–December 2026 is due on 13 January 2027.

What is the late fee for filing GSTR-1 late? ₹50 a day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), or ₹20 a day if you had no outward supplies. Under Notification 20/2021-CT the total is capped at ₹500 for a nil return, ₹2,000 for aggregate turnover up to ₹1.5 crore and ₹5,000 for ₹1.5–5 crore. Above ₹5 crore the section 47(1) maximum of ₹10,000 (₹5,000 + ₹5,000) applies.

Does the 57th GST Council late fee waiver apply to GSTR-1? No. The Council's 8 October 2026 recommendation is about the return under section 39(1), which is GSTR-3B, for turnover up to ₹5 crore if filed by the end of the due month. GSTR-1 is filed under section 37, so it is not covered as worded. And as of 15 November 2026 we found no notification bringing even the GSTR-3B waiver into force.

I missed the IFF for November. What now? IFF cannot be filed after the 13th. Report those B2B invoices in your quarterly GSTR-1 for October–December 2026, due 13 January 2027. Your buyers will see the credit later, so tell anyone waiting for it.

Can I fix a mistake in a filed GSTR-1? Yes. You can amend details through GSTR-1A before you file GSTR-3B for that period, or in a later GSTR-1.
