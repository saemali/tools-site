Rates and caps used

  • Late fee: ₹25/day CGST + ₹25/day SGST (₹50/day); nil returns ₹10 + ₹10 (₹20/day). No late fee under IGST.
  • Caps per return (CGST + SGST): nil return ₹500; turnover up to ₹1.5 cr ₹2,000; ₹1.5–5 cr ₹5,000; above ₹5 cr ₹10,000. Same caps for GSTR-3B (Notification 19/2021-CT) and GSTR-1 (Notification 20/2021-CT).
  • Interest (s.50 CGST Act): 18% a year on tax paid late, on the portion paid in cash (Notification 13/2017-CT), per day from the day after the due date.

Official sources: GST portal: GSTR-3B late fee and interest, Notification 19/2021-CT, Notification 20/2021-CT, Notification 13/2017-CT, CGST Act s.50 (CBIC).

Common questions · aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

What is the late fee for GSTR-3B?

₹50 a day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), or ₹20 a day for a nil return, capped by turnover: ₹2,000 up to ₹1.5 crore, ₹5,000 up to ₹5 crore, ₹10,000 above, and ₹500 for nil returns.

What interest is charged on late GST?

18% a year under section 50 of the CGST Act, on tax paid late in cash, from the day after the due date until payment.

Is there a late fee on IGST?

No. Late fee is charged only under CGST and SGST/UTGST.
