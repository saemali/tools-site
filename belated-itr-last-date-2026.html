Belated ITR Last Date 31 Dec 2026: Late Fee Calculator (AY 2026-27)

Missed the ITR due date for FY 2025-26? You have not lost your chance. You can still file a belated return until 31 December 2026. It costs a fixed late fee plus interest on any tax still unpaid, and every week of delay adds interest, so filing soon is cheaper.

AY 2026-27 is still governed by the Income-tax Act, 1961, even though the Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026 (CBDT transition FAQs, Q3.7 and Q3.8). The Finance Act, 2026 extended only the revised-return window; the belated-return limit stays 31 December.

Interest under Section 234A is 1% per month or part of a month on unpaid tax, counted from the day after the due date to the filing date. This is a simple estimate; the e-filing portal computes the exact figure, and 234B/234C interest on advance tax may also apply.

5-step checklist before you file

  1. Download Form 26AS and AIS from incometax.gov.in and match every TDS entry and interest income.
  2. Pay the 234F fee and any balance tax as self-assessment tax (challan 280) before you submit.
  3. In the ITR form, choose filing section 139(4) – belated.
  4. Note that the new tax regime applies by default; the old regime could only be chosen by the original due date.
  5. e-Verify within 30 days (Aadhaar OTP or net banking), or the return is treated as not filed.

What you lose by filing late

  • Business and capital losses of the year cannot be carried forward.
  • Refunds arrive later, and interest on refund is reduced for the delay.

Common questions · aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

What is the last date to file a belated ITR for AY 2026-27?

31 December 2026, under Section 139(4) of the Income-tax Act, 1961, or before your assessment is completed if that happens earlier.

How much is the late fee for a belated return?

Section 234F charges a flat ₹1,000 if total income is up to ₹5 lakh and ₹5,000 above that. It does not grow with the number of days late.

Can I revise a belated return?

Yes. From AY 2026-27 a revised return can be filed up to 31 March 2027. A revision filed after 31 December 2026 attracts a new Section 234-I fee of ₹1,000 or ₹5,000.

What if I miss 31 December 2026 too?

You can no longer file a regular return for AY 2026-27. The remaining route is an updated return (ITR-U) under Section 139(8A), which needs additional tax of 25% to 70% of the extra tax.

Is there a late fee if my income is below the basic exemption limit?

If you were not required to file, a voluntary late return does not attract the 234F fee. If you had to file (for example, income above the exemption limit), the fee applies even when TDS covers all your tax.
