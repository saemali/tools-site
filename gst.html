How GST is calculated

Add GST: GST = amount × rate ÷ 100. Remove GST: base = amount × 100 ÷ (100 + rate).

Within your state the tax splits equally into CGST and SGST. For a client in another state it's charged as IGST. Since 22 Sep 2025 the main slabs are 0%, 5%, 18% and 40%, and most professional services are at 18%. Source: CBIC, PIB FAQ.

Common questions · aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

When does a freelancer need GST registration?

Generally when annual turnover from services crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). The PIB FAQ on the 2025 rate changes confirms the thresholds were not changed.

Do I charge GST to foreign clients?

Exports of services can be zero-rated if you file a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) each financial year. Confirm eligibility with a CA.

What happened to the 12% and 28% slabs?

From 22 Sep 2025 most items moved to 5% or 18%, with 40% for specified luxury and sin goods.
