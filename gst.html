How GST is calculated

Add GST: GST = amount × rate / 100. Remove GST: base = amount × 100 / (100 + rate). For sales within your state, GST splits equally into CGST and SGST. For sales to another state, the whole amount is IGST.

Do freelancers need GST?

Service providers generally need to register once their annual turnover crosses ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh in special category states). Exports of services can be zero-rated under a LUT. Always confirm with a CA.
