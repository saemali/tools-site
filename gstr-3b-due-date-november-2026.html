GSTR-3B Due Date November 2026: 20 Nov for Monthly Filers, PMT-06 by 25 Nov for QRMP

Worried you'll miss the GSTR-3B date this month? Take a breath. For the October 2026 period there are only two dates, and which one is yours depends on how you file. Here they are, with what happens if you're late and a short checklist to get it done calmly.

Ghabraiye mat: apni date pehchaniye, 2B check kijiye, aur 20 se pehle file kar dijiye.

The quick answer

Who you are What is due Due date
Monthly filer GSTR-3B for October 2026 (file the return and pay the tax) Friday, 20 November 2026
QRMP filer October tax through challan PMT-06 (no GSTR-3B this month) Wednesday, 25 November 2026
QRMP filer Quarterly GSTR-3B for October–December 2026 22 or 24 January 2027, by state (not due in November)

The GST portal's GSTR-3B user guide says the monthly GSTR-3B is due on the 20th day of the month after the tax period, and the quarterly one on the 22nd or 24th day of the month after the quarter, depending on the state or UT. The Government can extend these dates by notification. As of 14 Nov 2026 we found no extension for the October 2026 period. The latest Central Tax notifications we could find (up to No. 07/2026-CT, dated 27 Oct 2026) deal with other changes, not return dates. We could not open CBIC's own notification list or the gst.gov.in news page directly while checking, so look at the "Upcoming Due Dates" box on gst.gov.in before you file.

Monthly or QRMP: which one are you?

  • Monthly filer: you file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month. Your October GSTR-3B is due 20 November.
  • QRMP (Quarterly Return, Monthly Payment): you file GSTR-3B once a quarter but pay tax every month. October is the first month of the October–December quarter, so there is no GSTR-3B to file in November. Instead, pay October's tax through challan PMT-06 by 25 November.

Not sure which one you are? Log in to gst.gov.in and open the Returns Dashboard. It shows your filing preference and your due dates.

QRMP filers: how the October payment works

CBIC Circular 143/13/2020-GST says QRMP filers pay the tax for each of the first two months of a quarter in Form GST PMT-06 by the 25th of the following month. When you create the challan, pick "Monthly payment for quarterly taxpayer". There are two ways to work out the amount:

  1. Fixed sum (35% challan): the portal pre-fills an amount based on the cash tax you paid in the last quarter. If you pay this by the due date, the circular says no interest is charged even if your actual October tax turns out higher, as long as you clear the full quarter's tax in the quarterly GSTR-3B on time.
  2. Self-assessment: you work out October's actual tax after input tax credit and pay that. If you underpay or pay late, interest under Section 50 applies to the shortfall.

You don't need to deposit anything if your cash or credit ledger already covers October's tax, or if your tax for October is nil (same circular).

What happens if I miss the date?

Missing it is fixable, but there are two separate costs.

  1. Late fee (for a late GSTR-3B). The GST portal works it out as days of delay × ₹25 a day under CGST plus ₹25 a day under SGST (₹50 a day in total), or ₹10 + ₹10 (₹20 a day) if your tax is nil. Under Notification 19/2021-CT the total is capped at ₹500 for a nil return, ₹2,000 if your aggregate turnover is up to ₹1.5 crore, and ₹5,000 for ₹1.5–5 crore (CGST + SGST together). Work out your exact figure with our free GST late fee and interest calculator.
  2. Interest at 18% a year on tax paid late in cash, under Section 50(1) of the CGST Act (rate set by Notification 13/2017-CT), from the day after the due date until you pay. This also applies to a late PMT-06 payment (Circular 143/13/2020-GST).

Example: ₹9,000 of GST paid 10 days late costs about ₹9,000 × 18% × 10 ÷ 365 ≈ ₹44 in interest, plus ₹500 late fee (₹50 × 10 days). Small, but it adds up, and a late return can also delay your next filings and your clients' credit.

Pre-filing checklist (10 minutes)

  1. GSTR-1 first. Your October GSTR-1 was due 11 November (monthly filers). GSTR-3B is auto-filled from it, so file GSTR-1 first if you haven't yet. See our GSTR-1 November 2026 guide.
  2. Reconcile with GSTR-2B. For monthly filers, the portal generates the October GSTR-2B on 14 November (GST portal FAQ). Match it with your purchase bills and claim only the credit that shows in it and that you're entitled to.
  3. Check your ITC. Leave out blocked credits, and chase any supplier whose invoice is missing from 2B. If you still have FY 2025-26 credit to claim, the last chance is 30 November 2026. See our ITC last date guide.
  4. Reverse charge. Bought a service where you pay the GST, like an advocate's fees? It goes in Table 3.1(d) and must be paid in cash.
  5. QRMP: IFF closed on 13 Nov. The optional IFF window for October invoices ran from 1 to 13 November. It cannot be filed after the 13th, so if you missed it, report those B2B invoices in your quarterly GSTR-1 (due 13 January 2027).
  6. Check the tax figure. Use our free GST calculator to work out or split GST. It runs in your browser, with no signup and nothing saved.
  7. Pay, then file. Make sure your cash ledger covers what ITC doesn't, then file with EVC or DSC. Download the filed return and keep it with your October records.

Tip: don't leave it to the evening of the 20th, when the portal is busiest. Filing on the 17th or 18th gives you time to fix surprises.

FAQ

What is the GSTR-3B due date for October 2026? For monthly filers, GSTR-3B for October 2026 is due on Friday, 20 November 2026 (the 20th of the following month, per the GST portal user guide). As of 14 November 2026 we found no extension notification for this period.

Do QRMP filers file GSTR-3B in November 2026? No. October is the first month of the October–December quarter, so QRMP filers do not file GSTR-3B for October. They pay October's tax (if any) through challan PMT-06 by 25 November 2026. The quarterly GSTR-3B for Oct–Dec 2026 is due on 22 or 24 January 2027, depending on the state.

What is the PMT-06 due date for October 2026? 25 November 2026. CBIC Circular 143/13/2020-GST says QRMP filers pay tax for each of the first two months of the quarter in PMT-06 by the 25th of the following month. If your cash or credit ledger already covers October's tax, or your tax is nil, you need not deposit anything.

What is the late fee for filing GSTR-3B late? ₹25 per day under CGST plus ₹25 per day under SGST (₹50 a day in total), or ₹10 + ₹10 (₹20 a day) for a nil return, per the GST portal. Under Notification 19/2021-CT the total is capped at ₹500 for nil returns, ₹2,000 if your aggregate turnover is up to ₹1.5 crore, and ₹5,000 for ₹1.5–5 crore (CGST + SGST together).

How much interest is charged on late GST payment? 18% a year on tax paid late in cash, under Section 50(1) of the CGST Act and Notification 13/2017-CT, counted from the day after the due date until you pay.

I missed the IFF on 13 November. What now? IFF for October closed on 13 November 2026 and cannot be filed after that. It was optional. Report those B2B invoices in your quarterly GSTR-1 for Oct–Dec, due 13 January 2027. Your buyers will see the credit later, so tell any buyer who was waiting for it.

Has the GSTR-3B due date for October 2026 been extended? As of 14 November 2026 we found no notification extending it. The latest Central Tax notifications we could find (up to 07/2026-CT, 27 October 2026) do not change return dates. Extensions come by CBIC notification and show on gst.gov.in, so check before you file.
