GSTR-1 Due Date November 2026: Checklist for Small Sellers

Diwali sales are done. Now the paperwork: every invoice from October has to be reported in GSTR-1. Here is the date, who it applies to, and a checklist so nothing slips.

Tax ki tension kam: Diwali ka hisaab, GSTR-1 mein saaf.

The dates

Who Period Due date
Monthly GSTR-1 filer October 2026 Wednesday, 11 November 2026
QRMP filer (optional IFF for B2B invoices) October 2026 (month 1 of the Oct–Dec quarter) Friday, 13 November 2026
QRMP filer, quarterly GSTR-1 October–December 2026 13 January 2027 (not due in November)

The GST portal's GSTR-1 guide says GSTR-1 is due on the 11th of the following month for monthly filers and the 13th of the month after the quarter for quarterly filers, unless extended by notification. QRMP filers may upload B2B invoices through IFF from the 1st to the 13th of the month after each of the first two months of the quarter (CGST Rule 59(2)).

Also in November: GSTR-3B for October is due 20 November 2026 for monthly filers (20th-of-the-month rule, GST portal).

Why GSTR-1 matters more than it looks

GSTR-1 is your sales statement. Your buyers' GSTR-2B, and so their input tax credit, is built from what you report. File late or get it wrong and your B2B customers cannot claim ITC. That is how small sellers lose repeat business.

Your GSTR-3B is also auto-filled from GSTR-1, so mistakes carry through.

Small seller's GSTR-1 checklist

Before you file 1. Export every October invoice, including cash sales above the B2C threshold and festive-offer bills. 2. Check invoice numbers run in order. Note any cancelled numbers in the document summary (Table 13). 3. B2B vs B2C. Has every B2B invoice got the buyer's correct GSTIN? 4. Place of supply. Inter-state sales are IGST. Same state is CGST + SGST. 5. Rates after GST 2.0. Since 22 Sep 2025 many goods moved to 5% / 18% / 40% (CBIC Notification 9/2025-IT(R)). Check your item master: an old 12% rate on a bill is a red flag. 6. HSN summary. AATO up to ₹5 crore: 4-digit HSN (mandatory on B2B, optional on B2C). Above ₹5 crore: 6-digit (Notification 78/2020-CT, from 1 Apr 2021). 7. Credit and debit notes for festive returns and discounts. 8. Advances received for orders not yet billed, if any.

Tools that help - Re-check any doubtful line in the free GST calculator. - Raise clean, numbered invoices with the free GST invoice maker. Fewer fixes at month-end.

After you file - Download the filed GSTR-1 PDF and keep it. - Missed something? Under the GST portal guide you can amend through GSTR-1A before filing that period's GSTR-3B, or in a later GSTR-1.

Late filing

Late fee is ₹50/day (₹25 CGST + ₹25 SGST), or ₹20/day for a nil return. It is capped at ₹500 for nil returns. For other returns the cap is ₹2,000 if AATO is up to ₹1.5 cr, ₹5,000 for ₹1.5–5 cr, and ₹10,000 above ₹5 cr (s.47 CGST Act; Notif. 20/2021-CT). More importantly, under sequential filing rules you generally cannot file a later return until the earlier one is done, so one delay spreads.

Worked example: a small shop's October GSTR-1

Sunita runs a gift shop. Her October sales: - 6 B2B invoices to registered resellers: ₹1,50,000 taxable - B2C counter sales in her own state: ₹3,00,000 taxable - One festive return: credit note of ₹10,000 taxable to a reseller

In GSTR-1 she reports the 6 B2B invoices one by one with each buyer's GSTIN, the B2C sales in summary, and the credit note against the original invoice. Net B2B taxable value: ₹1,40,000.

If she types one reseller's GSTIN wrong, that reseller's ITC goes to someone else's GSTR-2B. That is a phone call nobody wants after Diwali.

Common GSTR-1 mistakes

  • Reporting a B2B sale as B2C because the buyer's GSTIN was not taken at billing.
  • Missing credit notes for returns, which overstates your tax.
  • Mismatched totals between GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B.
  • Wrong place of supply on inter-state orders.

Raising every bill in the invoice maker with the GSTIN captured at billing time removes most of these problems.

A simple monthly routine

  • 1st–5th: export last month's invoices and credit notes.
  • 6th–8th: check GSTINs, place of supply and rates, and fix any errors.
  • 9th–10th: upload and preview GSTR-1, and compare its totals with your books.
  • By the 11th: file, and download the acknowledgement.

Put these dates in your phone calendar as repeating reminders. Filing a day or two early keeps you clear of last-day portal slowdowns and leaves time to fix surprises.

FAQ

What is the GSTR-1 due date for October 2026? For monthly filers, GSTR-1 for October 2026 is due on 11 November 2026 (11th of the following month, per the GST portal), unless extended.

When do QRMP filers file GSTR-1? Quarterly GSTR-1 is due on the 13th of the month after the quarter, so 13 January 2027 for Oct–Dec 2026. In the first two months of the quarter, QRMP filers can optionally report B2B invoices through IFF.

What happens if I file GSTR-1 late? A late fee applies per day of delay, your B2B buyers' ITC is delayed, and sequential filing rules can hold up your later returns.

Can I correct a mistake in a filed GSTR-1? Yes. The GST portal allows amendments through GSTR-1A before filing GSTR-3B for that period, or in a subsequent GSTR-1.

Is GSTR-1 required if I had no sales? Yes, registered taxpayers generally have to file a nil GSTR-1 for periods with no supplies.
