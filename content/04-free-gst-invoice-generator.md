## What a GST tax invoice must contain

Rule 46 of the CGST Rules lists what goes on a tax invoice. For a typical freelancer or small seller, that means:

1. Supplier's **name, address and GSTIN**
2. A **unique, consecutive invoice number** (up to 16 characters; letters, numbers, "-" and "/" are fine)
3. **Date of issue**
4. Recipient's **name, address and GSTIN** (if they are registered)
5. **HSN code** (goods) or **SAC code** (services)
6. **Description**, quantity and unit (for goods)
7. **Taxable value**, after any discount
8. **Rate and amount** of CGST + SGST, or IGST
9. **Place of supply** (with state name) for inter-state supplies
10. Whether tax is payable on **reverse charge**
11. **Signature** or digital signature

Rule 46 also requires: (a) total value of supply; (b) for an **unregistered** recipient where the value is ₹50,000 or more: name, address, delivery address and state name/code; (c) delivery address if different from place of supply; (d) QR code/IRN if you are e-invoicing.

## Sample GST invoice format (services)

```
TAX INVOICE
Invoice No: HM/2026-27/014        Date: 27-10-2026
From: Priya Sharma Design, Pune, Maharashtra
GSTIN: 27XXXXX0000X1ZX   PAN: XXXXX0000X
To:   Acme Pvt Ltd, Mumbai, Maharashtra
GSTIN: 27YYYYY0000Y1ZY
Place of supply: Maharashtra (27)

| # | Description            | SAC    | Amount (₹) |
|---|------------------------|--------|-----------:|
| 1 | Brand identity design  | 998391 | 40,000.00  |
            Taxable value            40,000.00
            CGST @ 9%                 3,600.00
            SGST @ 9%                 3,600.00
            TOTAL                    47,200.00
Amount in words: Forty-seven thousand two hundred rupees only
Reverse charge: No
Bank: XXXX | UPI: name@bank
Authorised signatory
```

SAC 998391 (specialty design services, including graphic, interior and fashion design) is a typical code for brand/graphic design. Still check the right SAC for your own service in the CBIC SAC list. For an out-of-state client, replace CGST + SGST with **IGST @ 18%**. Most freelance services are at 18% (see our [GST rates for freelancers](02-gst-rate-for-freelancers-2026.md) guide).

## Not GST-registered? Use a "bill of supply" or simple invoice

If you are below the registration threshold (₹20 lakh for services in most states, under Section 22), you **cannot charge GST**. Issue a plain invoice with no GST lines, and do not show a GSTIN you do not have.

## Common mistakes that cause trouble

- **Repeating or skipping invoice numbers.** Keep one series for each financial year.
- **Charging CGST+SGST to an out-of-state client.** That should be IGST.
- **Wrong client GSTIN.** Your client loses ITC and you will hear about it.
- **"Inclusive" pricing.** It eats into your fee. Quote "+ GST".
- **E-invoicing.** E-invoicing is mandatory if your aggregate turnover exceeded **₹5 crore** in any financial year from 2017-18 (Notification 10/2023-CT, from 1 Aug 2023). Most freelancers are far below it.

## Worked example: same job, two clients

You are in Maharashtra and do ₹25,000 of work for each of two clients.

**Client in Mumbai (same state):**
- Taxable value: ₹25,000
- CGST 9%: ₹2,250
- SGST 9%: ₹2,250
- **Total: ₹29,500**

**Client in Delhi (another state):**
- Taxable value: ₹25,000
- IGST 18%: ₹4,500
- **Total: ₹29,500**

The total is the same, but the tax lines are different. Mix them up and your client's ITC will not match, and you will have to issue a correction.

## Worked example: a discount on the invoice

You quote ₹30,000 and give a 10% festive discount on the invoice. GST is charged on the **reduced** amount:
- ₹30,000 − ₹3,000 = ₹27,000 taxable
- GST 18% = ₹4,860
- **Total ₹31,860**, not ₹32,400.

## Numbering that never breaks

Use a format such as **PREFIX/FY/serial**, for example `HM/2026-27/001`. Start again at 001 each April. If you cancel an invoice, keep the number in your records marked "cancelled" rather than reusing it.

## Keep a copy of everything

Save every PDF in one folder for each financial year, named by invoice number. When you file GSTR-1 or answer a client query, you can find any invoice in seconds. Because the [invoice maker](https://saemali.github.io/tools-site/invoice.html) does not store anything, your folder is your record.

## Before you hit send

Check three things: the client's GSTIN matches their certificate, the invoice number follows on from your last one, and the total in words matches the total in figures. Thirty seconds of checking saves a week of back-and-forth over a revised invoice.

## FAQ

**How can I make a GST invoice for free?**
Use a browser-based tool such as the HisaabMitra GST invoice maker. Enter your details, the client's details and the line items, and it calculates GST and lets you download a PDF with no signup.

**What details are mandatory on a GST invoice?**
Under Rule 46 of the CGST Rules: supplier name, address and GSTIN, a unique serial number, date, recipient details, HSN/SAC, description, taxable value, GST rate and amount, place of supply, reverse charge status and a signature.

**Can I issue a GST invoice without GST registration?**
No. If you are not registered you cannot charge GST. Issue a plain invoice without GST.

**Should I charge IGST or CGST + SGST?**
CGST + SGST when you and the client are in the same state (intra-state). IGST when the client is in another state (inter-state).

**Is e-invoicing mandatory for freelancers?**
Only if your aggregate turnover is above the notified e-invoicing threshold, which is far above most freelancers' turnover. Check the current CBIC notification.
