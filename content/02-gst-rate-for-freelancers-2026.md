# New GST Slabs 2026 (0/5/18/40%): What Freelancers Charge

Since September 2025 you have probably seen "GST is now just 5% and 18%" in WhatsApp forwards. So should a freelancer now charge 5%? Short answer: **almost certainly not.** Here is what actually changed and what it means for your invoices.

*Tax ki tension kam: slab badle hain, par freelancer ka hisaab simple hai. Chalo samajhte hain.*

## What changed with "GST 2.0"

On the GST Council's recommendation, CBIC issued new rate notifications dated 17 September 2025, effective **22 September 2025**. For **goods**, Notification No. 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate) now lists the main slabs as:

| Slab | What it is for (broadly) |
|---|---|
| 0% / exempt | Essentials such as fresh food (separate exemption notifications) |
| **5%** | Everyday goods (Schedule I) |
| **18%** | Standard rate (Schedule II) |
| **40%** | Luxury and "sin" goods (Schedule III) |

The notification also has a few special rates (3%, 0.25%, 1.5%) and a 28% schedule for certain items. The old 12% and 28% headline slabs are largely gone for most goods.

**Important:** this notification is about **goods**. Services have their own rate notifications, which were also amended on the same date.

## So what GST rate does a freelancer charge?

Professional, technical and business services (SAC heading 9983) are taxed at **18%** (9% CGST + 9% SGST, or 18% IGST). The GST 2.0 changes effective 22 Sep 2025 (Notification 15/2025-Central Tax (Rate)) kept this rate at 9% CGST.

Example: you invoice a Pune client ₹40,000 for a website from Mumbai.
- CGST 9% = ₹3,600
- SGST 9% = ₹3,600
- **Invoice total = ₹47,200**

If the same client were in Bengaluru, you would charge IGST 18% = ₹7,200 instead. Same total, different label. Our free [GST calculator](https://saemali.github.io/tools-site/gst.html) does this split instantly, and it also works backwards from a GST-inclusive price.

## Do I even need GST registration?

Under Section 22 of the CGST Act, registration is required once your **aggregate turnover in a financial year crosses ₹20 lakh** for service providers. In certain special-category states the limit is **₹10 lakh**. If you are below that and only serve Indian clients, you usually do not charge GST at all, and you should **not** put GST on your invoice if you are not registered.

Some situations can require registration whatever your turnover (for example, certain inter-state or e-commerce cases). If that might be you, ask a CA.

## Foreign clients: 0%, but with paperwork

Work for a client abroad, paid in foreign exchange, can qualify as **export of services**, which is "zero-rated". To invoice without paying IGST, a registered freelancer files a **Letter of Undertaking (LUT)** in Form GST RFD-11 on the GST portal, ideally before the financial year's first export invoice. LUTs are filed for each financial year.

Note: export turnover still counts towards the ₹20 lakh registration threshold.

## What to put on a freelancer GST invoice

- Your name, address and GSTIN
- Client name, address, and GSTIN if they have one
- Invoice number and date
- Description of the service and its SAC code
- Taxable value, GST rate, and CGST/SGST or IGST amounts
- Place of supply
- For exports under LUT, the endorsement required by CGST Rule 46: "SUPPLY MEANT FOR EXPORT/SUPPLY TO SEZ UNIT OR SEZ DEVELOPER FOR AUTHORISED OPERATIONS UNDER BOND OR LETTER OF UNDERTAKING WITHOUT PAYMENT OF INTEGRATED TAX"

## Pricing tip: quote "+ GST", not "inclusive"

If you are registered, quote ₹40,000 + GST. If you quote ₹40,000 "all-inclusive", the GST comes out of your pocket: your real fee drops to ₹33,898. Small habit, big difference over a year.

## Quick recap

- Goods slabs moved to **5% / 18% / 40%** from **22 Sep 2025** (CBIC Notification 9/2025-IT(R)).
- Most freelance services: **18%** (Notification 11/2017-CT(Rate) as amended by 15/2025-CT(Rate)).
- Registration: above **₹20 lakh** (₹10 lakh in special-category states).
- Foreign clients: zero-rated with an **LUT**.

*Seedhi baat: 18% lagao, invoice sahi banao, aur LUT time pe bharo. Tax ki tension kam, kaam pe focus zyada.*

## Worked example: the real cost of "inclusive" pricing over a year

Meera, a registered designer, bills ₹50,000 a month. Here is her year under each way of quoting:

| | "₹50,000 + GST" | "₹50,000 inclusive" |
|---|---|---|
| Client pays monthly | ₹59,000 | ₹50,000 |
| GST paid to government | ₹9,000 | ₹7,627 |
| **Meera keeps monthly** | **₹50,000** | **₹42,373** |
| Kept over 12 months | ₹6,00,000 | ₹5,08,475 |

The difference is **₹91,525 a year**, from one word on the quote. Work out an inclusive figure in reverse with the [GST calculator](https://saemali.github.io/tools-site/gst.html): divide by 1.18 to get the base amount.

## Common mistakes freelancers make with GST

- **Adding GST before registering.** You cannot collect it without a GSTIN.
- **Charging CGST + SGST to an out-of-state client.** That should be IGST.
- **Exporting without an LUT.** You end up paying IGST up front that you did not need to.
- **Spending the GST you collect.** Keep it in a separate account until GSTR-3B is filed.

## Worked example: a mixed month

In November, Meera bills a Pune client ₹30,000, a Delhi client ₹20,000 and a US client ₹25,000 (an export under LUT). Her GST lines:
- Pune: CGST ₹2,700 + SGST ₹2,700
- Delhi: IGST ₹3,600
- US: ₹0 (zero-rated under LUT)

GST collected: **₹9,000**, all of which goes into that month's GSTR-3B. Her turnover for the registration threshold is the full ₹75,000.

## FAQ

**What is the GST rate for freelancers in 2026?**
Most freelance services such as design, development, writing and consulting attract 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%, or IGST 18%). The 5/18/40% slab change from 22 September 2025 mainly applied to goods. Check the rate for your specific SAC code.

**Is GST registration required for freelancers?**
Registration is mandatory once aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds ₹20 lakh for service providers (₹10 lakh in certain special-category states), under Section 22 of the CGST Act. Some cases need registration whatever the turnover.

**Do freelancers charge GST to foreign clients?**
Services that qualify as exports are zero-rated. A registered freelancer can file a Letter of Undertaking (Form GST RFD-11) on the GST portal and invoice without paying IGST.

**Did GST 2.0 reduce GST on services to 5%?**
No, not for typical freelance services. The headline 5/18/40% slabs came from goods rate notifications such as Notification 9/2025-Integrated Tax (Rate). Standard professional services remain at 18%.

**Can I charge GST if I am not registered?**
No. Only registered persons can collect GST. If you are below the threshold and not registered, do not add GST to your invoice.
