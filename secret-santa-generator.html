How it works

  1. Enter everyone's name, one per line.
  2. Add exclusions for couples, siblings or anyone who already buys for each other.
  3. Set one clear budget so nobody overspends.
  4. Press Draw names. The tool picks a random valid draw: nobody gets themselves and no excluded pair is matched. If no valid draw exists, it tells you why instead of cheating.
  5. Send each person their match with Copy message (paste into WhatsApp or email), or print and cut the slips.

Tips for a warm, inclusive Secret Santa

  • Agree on one budget, and keep it modest. A clear cap (for example ₹500 or $10) is fairer to interns, students and anyone on a tight month. Say "up to", not "around".
  • Make joining optional. In an office, ask rather than assume. Someone may not celebrate Christmas or may be saving money, and that's fine. "Year-end gift swap" works as a name too.
  • Ask for a short wish list. Three ideas and one "please no" (food allergy, no alcohol, vegetarian, no fragrances) avoids awkward gifts.
  • Keep office gifts neutral. Skip alcohol, jokes about someone's looks or age, and anything too personal.
  • Plan for remote colleagues. Set a ship-by date, or allow an e-gift card of the same value.
  • Set the reveal day early. Put the exchange date in the event box so it appears in every message.

Gift ideas under ₹500 / $10

  • A small indoor plant (money plant, succulent) in a nice pot
  • A good coffee or tea sampler, or a box of local sweets or chocolates
  • A notebook or planner and a nice pen
  • A mug with a packet of hot chocolate
  • A paperback by an author they like (check their wish list)
  • A card game such as UNO for the team break room
  • Cosy socks, a phone stand or a cable organiser
  • Homemade cookies or a jar of masala chai mix, with a handwritten tag

Secret Santa questions

Is this Secret Santa generator really free?

Yes. There is no signup, no email and no limit on how many times you draw.

Do you see or store the names I enter?

No. The draw runs in JavaScript in your own browser. Names are not sent to any server, saved or tracked. Close the tab and they are gone.

Can I stop couples or family members drawing each other?

Yes. In the exclusions box, put each couple or family on its own line, for example "Asha & Ravi". Everyone on a line is kept apart in both directions.

Why does it say no draw is possible?

Usually one excluded group is bigger than half of all participants, or someone is excluded from everyone. Remove an exclusion or add more people and try again.

How can the organiser take part without seeing everyone's match?

Don't press Reveal for others. Use "Copy message" next to each name and paste it straight into a private WhatsApp, SMS or email to that person, or print the slips and fold them without looking.

What is a good Secret Santa budget in India?

For offices and families, ₹300 to ₹500 is common and fair for most people. Whatever you choose, state one clear maximum so nobody overspends or feels awkward.
