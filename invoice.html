What must a GST invoice include?

Supplier name, address and GSTIN, invoice number and date, recipient details, SAC/HSN code, taxable value, GST rate and amount, and place of supply.

Can I use this if I'm not GST registered?

Choose 0% and the generator makes a regular invoice. Don't charge GST unless you are registered.
