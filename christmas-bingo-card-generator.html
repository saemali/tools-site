How to run Christmas bingo (5 minutes to set up)

  1. Make one card per player, plus two spares. For a big group use the Christmas classic list (75 words) so cards don’t all fill up together.
  2. Print the cards at 100% scale on plain paper or light card. Four per page saves paper for a classroom; one per page is easier for grandparents to read.
  3. Print the caller sheet, or just press Call next word on this page. Cut out the call slips if you’d rather draw words from a hat.
  4. Agree what wins before you start: a line (row, column or diagonal), four corners, or a full card for the final round.
  5. Check the winner against the called words. Keep the set code if you might need to reprint the same cards.

Ideas that make it more fun

  • Small prizes: a chocolate, a candy cane, first pick in the gift exchange, or skipping the washing-up.
  • Office party: use the office list or type in friendly in-jokes from your own team. Pair it with a Secret Santa draw.
  • Little kids: choose 3×3 and the kids list, and let them use stickers instead of pens.
  • Keep the season going: add bingo night to a free advent activity calendar, or send the kids on a treasure hunt for their presents with the free Christmas scavenger hunt clue generator.

Bingo card questions

Is the bingo card generator free?

Yes. There is no signup, no email, no watermark and no limit. Make up to 200 different cards at a time and print as many copies as you like for your own party, class or office.

Are all the cards different?

Yes. No two cards in a set have the same group of words (or, for number bingo, the same grid). If your word list is too short to make that many different cards, the tool tells you and asks for more words.

How many words do I need?

A 5×5 card with a free centre needs 24 different words, a 4×4 card needs 16 and a 3×3 card with a free centre needs 8. Use more words than that (40 or more for a big group) so that cards don't fill up at the same time.

Can I get the same cards again later?

Yes. Each set has a short set code. Type the same code, words and settings again and you get exactly the same cards and calling order, which helps if you need to reprint one.

How do I play Christmas bingo?

Give each player a card and a pen. The caller reads the words from the caller sheet (or presses Call next word on this page) and players mark any they have. The first to complete a full row, column or diagonal shouts Bingo and the caller checks their card against the called words.

Is my word list sent anywhere?

No. The cards are made by JavaScript in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or saved by us.
