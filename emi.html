The EMI formula

EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan, r the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n the number of months. Banks may round slightly differently or add processing fees.

Common questions · aksar pooche jaane wale sawaal

Does a longer tenure save money?

It lowers the EMI but increases the total interest you pay.

Is this the exact bank EMI?

It uses the standard reducing-balance formula. Your bank's figure may differ by a few rupees due to rounding and fees.
